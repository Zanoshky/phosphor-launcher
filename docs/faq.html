FAQ and troubleshooting

Quick answers. If yours is not here, report it.

questions

Is pro a second charge I need?

No. The Play price buys the whole app, yours to keep. Pro is one optional purchase inside it, for those who want widgets, 2fa codes, news, gps and the other extras. No subscription. See phosphor pro.

I bought pro on my old phone.

Open settings, phosphor pro, and tap restore purchase.

How do I try the ten looks?

Open look, presets, and tap one. my own look brings back what you had. See the recipe.

Where did the glass setting go?

It is gone since 1.65. The home is solid now, and a picture shows at the strength its preset was made for. The free glass CRT is still in look, crt.

Where did trinitron, slot mask and the other tubes go?

They were hard to read, so they were removed. If you used one, you get the free glass instead. Pro keeps amber, green, vhs and curved.

Where did the passwords panel and the editor go?

They are part of phosphor pro now.

Why does the lock gesture need accessibility?

Android lets an app lock the screen only through an accessibility service. The Phosphor lock service only locks the screen, and it is off until you turn it on.

Can I name my pages?

Yes. Hold empty space, pages, name, or type :rename. See the recipe.

Does the gps panel send my location?

No. The gps panel (pro) uses your precise location only if you allow it, and it is not sent to us.

Where are the settings?

Tap ◆ phosphor at the top left. If the top bar is hidden (it is on a fresh install), hold empty space and tap settings in the strip. You can also swipe up and type :settings.

Can Phosphor replace my lock screen?

Android does not allow that. Phosphor draws a live wallpaper that shows behind Android's own lock screen, in the same look as home. Android still asks for your PIN or fingerprint.

I see two clocks on the lock screen.

The second one is Android's. Use the setup step system lock cards: set lock screen notifications to "Don't show any notifications" and turn off "Dynamic clock".

Which Android version do I need?

Android 7 or newer.

Does it need the internet?

Only for the features you use: the weather and the city lookup when you type a place, the radio while a station plays, and the panels you add that fetch from the web, like air quality, holidays, news, radar or git, while they show. Everything else works offline. The privacy policy lists every host and what it receives.

Does it collect my data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Why does the real visualizer ask for the microphone?

Android uses the microphone permission for reading the sound the phone plays. Phosphor only reads how loud each band is, to move the bars. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent.

Can the theme change at night?

Yes. Open look, day and night ›, set switch to by the sun or 22 to 7, and pick a day theme and a night theme. By the sun uses sunrise and sunset where the weather is, worked out on the phone. See the recipe.

Can I make a row of app icons?

Yes, with the app shelf panel: one app per cell, as a 4x1 row, a 1x4 column, a 2x2 grid or bigger. See the recipe.

Does live weather cost battery?

A little. In rain it costs about the same as the rain background. Under a clear sky it draws nothing. It shows on the home screen only.

Does day and night send my location anywhere?

No. Sunrise and sunset are worked out on the phone, from the place the weather already uses.

Which weather style saves the most battery?

Big numbers and waybar. Live sky is the only style that moves, so it costs the most. Change it in the weather panel's menu, style. See the recipe.

Can I use my own font?

Phosphor uses your phone's own monospace font. It does not bundle fonts.

How many pages can I have?

Up to ten.

How do I go back to my old home screen?

In Android settings, pick it as the Home app. On most phones that is under Apps, then Default apps.

troubleshooting

The weather says tap to allow, or shows nothing.

Allow location, or type a city under settings, clock and weather, weather places. If it says offline, check your connection and tap the panel to try again.

The notifications panel is empty.

Phosphor needs notification access. Open the setup guide and use step 3, allow access.

Media controls do not show.

Now playing appears only while a player has a session, and it needs notification access.

The calendar or meeting panel shows nothing.

Allow the calendar. The meeting page offers allow calendar when it has no access.

The lock screen went back to a plain wallpaper.

Android can drop a live wallpaper when the app is stopped or updated. Set it again: settings, phone, lock screen, set as lock screen.

The radio does not play.

The radio needs the internet. If a station is down, pick another one. Errors show in plain words, like offline or station not answering.

Text is too small or too big.

Change text size in look: small, medium or large. Phosphor also follows your phone's font size, up to 200%.

I removed a panel by mistake.

Tap undo in the strip within 6 seconds. Or add it again with + add.

I changed too much and want a fresh start.

Settings, help, reset to defaults. Your settings are saved first, so bring back the saved copy can undo it.

Motion looks choppy, or the battery drains fast.

Check frame rate in settings, motion and battery. 60 is smooth, 30 saves battery. Turning the wallpaper off in look saves the most; terrain is the heaviest background. If it still looks wrong, report a bug with your phone model.
